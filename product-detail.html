Product Description

Factory Finish is a high-performance finishing compound designed for professional applications. It delivers long-lasting gloss and durability.

Directions

  1. Ensure surface is clean, dry and free of dust.
  2. Apply evenly with a compatible applicator.
  3. Allow at least 2 hours between coats.
  4. Store in a cool, well-ventilated place.

Warnings

Warning: Flammable. Keep away from heat, sparks and open flame. Avoid breathing vapours. Use in well-ventilated areas. Keep out of reach of children.

Eco Information

Packaging is recyclable. Rinse containers before recycling. Contains low levels of volatile compounds; follow local disposal guidelines.
