Factory Finish — Standard

High-gloss finishing compound for professional use.

What it does

Delivers a deep, glossy finish designed to resist yellowing and wear. Ideal for bikes, plastics and painted surfaces.

Directions

  1. Clean surface and remove loose dirt.
  2. Apply with cloth or foam, buff evenly.
  3. Allow to cure for 24 hours for full hardness.

Safety

Keep away from children. Avoid inhalation and contact with eyes. Read the label PDF for full instructions.
